6.1.1 · DISCRETE ENTROPY

平均有多意外？How surprising is an outcome, on average?

一个必然发生的结果没有惊喜。一个概率很小的结果出现时，信息量很大。把一次结果的惊讶程度写成 −log p(x)，再按它真正发生的概率取平均，就得到熵。熵描述整个分布，而不是某一个结果

A certain outcome carries no surprise. A rare outcome carries more information when it occurs. Define the surprisal of one outcome as −log p(x), then average under the true probabilities. Entropy describes the whole distribution, rather than one particular observation.

\[I(x)=-\log_2 p(x),\qquad H(X)=H(p)=-\sum_{k=1}^{K}p_k\log_2 p_k=\mathbb E_p[I(X)]\]

以 2 为底时单位是 bits，以 e 为底时单位是 nats；两种单位换算为 Hbits=Hnats/ln 2。约定 0 log 0=0，因为概率为零的结果不会贡献平均值

Base-2 logarithms give bits; natural logarithms give nats. Convert by Hbits=Hnats/ln 2. Use 0 log 0=0: an impossible outcome contributes nothing to the average.

\[0\le H(X)\le\log_2 K,\qquad H(\theta)=-\theta\log_2\theta-(1-\theta)\log_2(1-\theta)\]

所有概率集中在一个状态时 H=0；K 个状态均匀时 H=log₂K。公平硬币 θ=0.5 时最难猜，熵是 1 bit；硬币越偏，长期越好猜，平均信息量越低。教材的五类例子 p=[0.25,0.25,0.2,0.15,0.15] 给出约 2.29 bits

Entropy is zero when all mass is on one state, and log₂K for K equally likely states. A fair coin at θ=0.5 is hardest to predict, with entropy 1 bit. A biased coin is more predictable on average. The book’s five-state example [0.25,0.25,0.2,0.15,0.15] has about 2.29 bits.

6.1.1.1–6.1.1.2 · APPLICATION & ESTIMATION

DNA 标志：哪些位置最有辨识度？DNA logos: which positions are informative?

把 N 条 DNA 序列对齐，在每个位置 t 数 A、C、G、T 的次数，再除以 N，得到该位置的经验概率向量 θ̂t，也叫 position weight matrix。字母高度可以只表示频率；sequence logo 则进一步表达该位置有多确定

Align N DNA sequences, count A, C, G, and T at each position t, and divide by N. The resulting empirical probabilities form a position weight matrix. A frequency display shows letter probabilities; a sequence logo additionally shows how conserved each position is.

\[\hat\theta_{tk}=\frac{N_{tk}}{N},\qquad H_t=-\sum_{k\in\{A,C,G,T\}}\hat\theta_{tk}\log_2\hat\theta_{tk},\qquad R_t=2-H_t\]

四种碱基均匀时 H=2、R=0，位置没有偏好；只有一个碱基时 H=0、R=2，位置高度保守。一个字母在 logo 中的高度是 θ̂tkRt。Consensus sequence 只保留每个位置最常见的字母，会丢掉概率与不确定性

Uniform bases give H=2 and R=0, with no positional preference. A deterministic base gives H=0 and R=2, a highly conserved position. A letter’s logo height is θ̂tkRt. A consensus sequence keeps only the most common letter at each position and discards uncertainty.

The four sliders are nonnegative weights, normalized automatically. The right-hand logo represents one position. All-zero weights fall back to a uniform distribution.

Estimation challenge: estimating entropy requires estimating probabilities. With many states and rare events, finite data miss part of the distribution. Long-tailed vocabulary and newly coined words are the book’s example. A plug-in estimate from a small sample need not accurately measure true entropy.

6.1.2 · CROSS ENTROPY

模型猜错分布，需要付出多少代价？What does a mismatched prediction cost?

p 是数据真正来自的分布，q 是模型给出的概率。真实样本仍按 p 出现，但你用 −log q(x) 计算代价，所以交叉熵用 p 来加权、用 q 来取对数。把“谁产生数据”和“谁负责预测”分开，是读懂公式的关键

p generates the data; q supplies model probabilities. Outcomes occur according to p, but their prediction cost is −log q(x). Cross entropy therefore weights by p and takes the logarithm of q. Keep the data source and predictor separate.

\[H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p,q)=-\sum_k p_k\log_2q_k,\qquad H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p,p)=H(p)\]

在理想编码意义下，正确的概率模型能达到源的熵；使用失配的 q 会增加平均编码代价。实际离散码长还受整数长度与分块编码限制，这里把交叉熵理解为理想的平均代价。它也正是机器学习中的平均 log loss

In ideal coding terms, a correct probability model reaches the source entropy; a mismatched q increases average cost. Actual discrete code lengths also involve integer lengths and block coding. Here cross entropy represents the ideal average cost, and it is also the average log loss used in learning.

先让 q=p，再让 p=0.9、q=0.1。真实数据很常出现的结果，被模型赋予很小概率，会带来很大的惩罚

First match q to p. Then set p=0.9 and q=0.1. A model is penalized heavily when it assigns a small probability to a common true outcome.

If pk>0 but qk=0, cross entropy is infinite. A term with pk=0 contributes zero. Cross entropy is generally asymmetric and usually remains positive even at q=p.

6.1.3–6.1.4 · JOINT & CONDITIONAL ENTROPY

一起猜，与知道一个后再猜Predicting together versus predicting after observation

联合熵 H(X,Y) 是同时确定 X 和 Y 的不确定性。条件熵 H(Y|X) 是先看到 X，再猜 Y 所剩的不确定性，并且要对所有可能的 X 取平均。不能把它等同于某次具体观察的熵 H(Y|X=x)

Joint entropy H(X,Y) measures uncertainty about the pair. Conditional entropy H(Y|X) measures uncertainty left about Y after observing X, averaged over all possible X values. It differs from the entropy for one particular observation H(Y|X=x).

\[H(X,Y)=-\sum_{x,y}p(x,y)\log_2p(x,y)\]
\[H(Y\mid X)=\sum_xp(x)H(Y\mid X=x)=H(X,Y)-H(X)\]
\[\max\{H(X),H(Y)\}\le H(X,Y)\le H(X)+H(Y)\]

教材从 1 到 8 均匀选整数：X 表示是否偶数，Y 表示是否质数。联合概率依次是 1/8、3/8、3/8、1/8；边缘分布都各半。因此 H(X)=H(Y)=1，H(X,Y)≈1.811，H(Y|X)≈0.811 bits

The book draws an integer uniformly from 1 through 8: X indicates evenness and Y primality. The four joint probabilities are 1/8, 3/8, 3/8, and 1/8; both marginals are balanced. Thus H(X)=H(Y)=1, H(X,Y)≈1.811, and H(Y|X)≈0.811 bits.

改变两个二元变量的关联Change the dependence of two binary variables

4

这个教学分布始终保持两个边缘概率各半。参数 r=P(Y=X)；r=0.5 时独立，r=0 或 1 时，一个变量完全决定另一个。教材例子对应 r=0.25

This teaching distribution keeps both marginals balanced. Set r=P(Y=X). At r=0.5 the variables are independent; at r=0 or 1, either variable fully determines the other. The book’s example has r=0.25.

H(X,Y) · bits—

链式法则：分步消除不确定性The chain rule: resolve uncertainty in stages

\[H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=\sum_{i=1}^nH(X_i\mid X_1,\ldots,X_{i-1})\]

两变量版本是 H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X)。独立时 H(Y|X)=H(Y)，联合熵相加；Y 是 X 的确定函数时 H(Y|X)=0，联合熵等于 H(X)

For two variables, H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X). Independence gives H(Y|X)=H(Y), so joint entropy adds. If Y is a deterministic function of X, then H(Y|X)=0 and joint entropy equals H(X).

“知道更多，熵更低”指平均：H(Y|X)≤H(Y)。某一个观察却可能增加不确定性。例如 X=a 的概率为 0.9，且此时 Y=0；X=b 的概率为 0.1，且此时 Y 各半。边缘 P(Y=1)=0.05，H(Y)≈0.286；看到 b 后熵是 1，但平均条件熵只有 0.1

Conditioning reduces uncertainty on average: H(Y|X)≤H(Y). A specific observation can increase it. Let P(X=a)=0.9 with Y=0, and P(X=b)=0.1 with balanced Y. Then P(Y=1)=0.05 and H(Y)≈0.286. Observing b gives entropy 1, yet the average conditional entropy is only 0.1.

6.1.5 · PERPLEXITY

把 bits 换成“有效选择数”Turn bits into an effective number of choices

困惑度把对数尺度的熵还原到选择数量的尺度：K 个等概率结果的熵是 log₂K，所以困惑度是 K。一个完全确定的分布困惑度是 1。非均匀分布得到的值可以不是整数

Perplexity turns log-scale entropy into an effective number of choices. K equally likely outcomes have entropy log₂K and perplexity K. A deterministic distribution has perplexity 1. Nonuniform distributions can have noninteger perplexity.

\[\operatorname{PPL}(p)=2^{H(p)},\qquad \operatorname{PPL}(p_D,q)=2^{H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p_D,q)}\]

评估语言模型时，计算模型对实际出现的每个 token 分配了多少概率。使用前面的上下文做条件，平均负对数概率，再指数化。不要误用模型自身的熵：测试集困惑度来自经验数据与模型的交叉熵

To evaluate a language model, look at the probability assigned to each observed token, conditioned on earlier context. Average the negative log probabilities and exponentiate. Test-set perplexity uses cross entropy between empirical data and the model, rather than the model’s own entropy.

\[\operatorname{PPL}=\exp\left[-\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N\ln q(x_n\mid x_{<n})\right]=\left(\prod_{n=1}^N\frac1{q(x_n\mid x_{<n})}\right)^{1/N}\]

四个实际 token 的预测概率Probabilities assigned to four observed tokens

5

每个滑块是不同位置上实际 token 的条件概率，并非同一个类别分布，所以它们不需要相加为 1。把一个概率降得很小，观察整体代价；这是倒数概率的几何平均

Each slider is the conditional probability of an observed token at a different position, not entries of one categorical distribution. They need not sum to one. Make one probability tiny and observe the cost: perplexity is a geometric mean of inverse probabilities.

语言模型中可把困惑度理解为有效 branching factor，即平均面对多少个等概率候选。如果真实过程本身有随机性，理想模型的期望困惑度也不会低于真实过程的 2ᴴ；有限测试集可能偏离这个期望。只有相同数据、分词方式和评估设置下的困惑度才适合比较

For language models, perplexity acts like an effective branching factor: the number of equally likely alternatives. An ideal model’s expected cross-entropy perplexity cannot beat 2ᴴ of the true stochastic process, though finite test data can deviate. Compare perplexities only under the same data, tokenization, and evaluation setup.

6.2.1–6.2.2 · RELATIVE ENTROPY

KL 是模型失配的额外代价KL is the extra cost of a mismatched model

\[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\parallel q)=\sum_kp_k\log\frac{p_k}{q_k}=\mathbb E_p\left[\log\frac{p(X)}{q(X)}\right]\]
\[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\parallel q)=H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p,q)-H(p)\]

交叉熵包含数据本来就有的随机性 H(p)，以及模型不匹配造成的额外代价 KL。固定 p 时，降低交叉熵就是降低 KL。使用 log₂ 时可解释为多付出的 bits；使用 ln 时单位是 nats

Cross entropy contains the source’s unavoidable randomness H(p), plus extra cost from model mismatch. With p fixed, reducing cross entropy reduces KL. Base-2 KL measures extra bits; natural-log KL measures nats.

\[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\parallel q)=\int p(x)\log\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\,dx\quad\text{(continuous)}\]

KL 也能比较连续密度，不需要学习已跳过的 differential entropy。注意 p∥q 的左边决定对谁取平均。交换顺序不仅改变分母，也改变权重

KL also compares continuous densities without requiring the skipped differential-entropy section. The left-hand distribution in p∥q determines the expectation. Reversing the order changes both the denominator and the weighting.

KL≥0，且等于零当且仅当分布相同（连续情形为几乎处处相同）。但 KL 一般不对称，也不满足三角不等式，因此是 divergence，而不是数学上的 metric。单个求和项可以是负的；非负性说的是总和

KL≥0, with equality exactly when the distributions agree (almost everywhere in the continuous case). It is generally asymmetric and does not satisfy the triangle inequality, so it is a divergence, not a metric. Individual terms may be negative; the full sum is nonnegative.

6.2.3 · GAUSSIAN KL

均值、方差不同，会怎样？What if means and variances differ?

对 p=N(μ₁,σ₁²)、q=N(μ₂,σ₂²)，KL 有闭式解。下面用标准差 σ 表示宽度，用自然对数计算 nats。均值差由 q 的方差来缩放，因此一个很窄的 q 对漏掉 p 的质量尤其敏感

For p=N(μ₁,σ₁²) and q=N(μ₂,σ₂²), KL has a closed form. Widths below are standard deviations σ, and natural logarithms give nats. The squared mean difference is scaled by q’s variance, making a narrow q particularly sensitive to mass it misses.

\[D(p\parallel q)=\ln\frac{\sigma_2}{\sigma_1}+\frac{\sigma_1^2+(\mu_1-\mu_2)^2}{2\sigma_2^2}-\frac12\]

6.2.4 · INFORMATION INEQUALITY

为什么 KL 不会小于零？Why can KL never be negative?

核心是 Jensen 不等式：凸函数满足 f(E[X])≤E[f(X)]；log 是凹函数，所以方向反过来。先设 A={x:p(x)>0}，只在 p 的支撑集上求和。如果其中有 q(x)=0，KL 已经是 ∞，无需继续证明

The key is Jensen’s inequality: a convex function satisfies f(E[X])≤E[f(X)]. Since log is concave, the direction reverses. Let A={x:p(x)>0} and sum on p’s support. If q is zero anywhere with positive p mass, KL is already infinite.

\[-D(p\parallel q)=\mathbb E_p\!\left[\log\frac{q(X)}{p(X)}\right]\le\log\mathbb E_p\!\left[\frac{q(X)}{p(X)}\right]=\log\sum_{x\in A}q(x)\le\log1=0\]

所以 −KL≤0，也就是 KL≥0。等号需要 q/p 在 A 上为常数，并且 q 的全部概率质量都在 A 内；结合归一化，就得到 p=q。取均匀分布 u=1/K，又得到 D(p∥u)=log₂K−H(p)≥0，证明均匀分布熵最大

Thus −KL≤0, or KL≥0. Equality requires a constant q/p on A and all of q’s mass inside A; normalization then gives p=q. Taking u=1/K yields D(p∥u)=log₂K−H(p)≥0, proving that a uniform distribution maximizes entropy.

可以回到交叉熵实验验证：无论怎样拖动 p、q，Hce(p,q)−H(p) 都不会为负；p=q 时才归零。数值验证帮助直觉，Jensen 才提供一般证明

Return to the cross-entropy experiment: Hce(p,q)−H(p) never becomes negative and vanishes only at p=q. Numerical examples build intuition; Jensen supplies the general proof.

6.2.5 · KL AND MAXIMUM LIKELIHOOD

最大似然是在匹配经验分布Maximum likelihood fits the empirical distribution

数据 D={x₁,…,xN} 的经验分布给每个观察 1/N 的质量。固定这个数据分布，熵项与模型参数 θ 无关，所以最小化交叉熵，等价于最小化平均负对数似然，再等价于最大化似然

The empirical distribution of D={x₁,…,xN} places mass 1/N on each observation. With the data fixed, its entropy term does not depend on model parameters θ. Minimizing cross entropy is therefore minimizing average negative log likelihood, or maximizing likelihood.

\[H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p_D,q_\theta)=-\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N\log q_\theta(x_n)\]
\[\arg\min_\theta D(p_D\parallel q_\theta)=\arg\max_\theta\sum_{n=1}^N\log q_\theta(x_n)\quad\text{(discrete case)}\]

离散硬币例子：10 次抛掷有 8 次正面，经验 P(head)=0.8，Bernoulli MLE 的 θ=0.8。上面的 p、q 实验固定 p=0.8，最小损失恰好出现在 q=0.8

For a discrete coin example, 8 heads in 10 tosses give empirical P(head)=0.8, and the Bernoulli MLE is θ=0.8. In the p,q experiment, fixing p=0.8 makes loss smallest at q=0.8.

连续数据的精确性：教材用 Dirac 点质量形式说明 MLE 关系。但离散经验点质量对光滑连续密度的严格 KL 通常是 ∞，不能把“∞ 加常数”当作有限目标。实际训练目标仍是样本平均 −log 密度；严格 KL 匹配可用平滑后的数据分布或合适的共同基准来说明

Continuous-data precision: the book presents the MLE connection using Dirac point masses. Strict KL from atomic empirical data to a smooth continuous distribution is generally infinite, so “infinity plus a constant” is not a finite objective. Training still uses sample-average negative log density; a rigorous KL interpretation can use a smoothed data distribution or an appropriate common reference.

经验分布只记录有限样本，不能代表所有未来情况。教材指出，MLE 可能过于关注训练点；data augmentation 将数据做合理扰动，帮助模型学习自然变化，而不只是记住这些样本

The empirical distribution records finitely many samples, rather than every future possibility. The book warns that likelihood training can focus too closely on training points. Data augmentation adds plausible perturbations so the model learns natural variation rather than only the observed examples.

6.2.6 · IMPORTANT

重点：谁在取平均，决定你怕漏掉什么Focus: the expectation determines what omission costs

把 p 看成目标、q 看成简化模型。两个方向都对 q 优化，但一个从目标 p 的视角检查，另一个从模型 q 的视角检查。它们面对的是不同的错误

Let p be the target and q the simplified approximation. Both optimize q, but one checks from the target’s perspective and the other from the approximation’s perspective. They penalize different errors.

\[\underbrace{D(p\parallel q)=\mathbb E_p[\log p-\log q]}_{\text{forward / inclusive KL}}\qquad\underbrace{D(q\parallel p)=\mathbb E_q[\log q-\log p]}_{\text{reverse / exclusive KL}}\]

Forward KL：别漏掉目标可能出现的区域Forward KL: do not miss plausible target regions

若 p(x)>0，但 q(x)=0，forward KL 就是 ∞。因为按 p 取平均，目标的每个重要峰都能“发声”。一个单峰 q 拟合双峰 p 时，往往会变宽以覆盖两边：zero-avoiding、mode-covering，也叫 inclusive KL；该投影称 M-projection / moment projection

If p(x)>0 but q(x)=0, forward KL is infinite. Averaging under p lets each important target mode contribute. A unimodal q approximating a bimodal p often widens to cover both: zero-avoiding, mode-covering, or inclusive KL. This is an M-projection, also called a moment projection.

Reverse KL：别把质量放到目标认为不可能的区域Reverse KL: avoid implausible target regions

若 q(x)>0，但 p(x)=0，reverse KL 就是 ∞。因为按 q 取平均，q 几乎不去的区域几乎没有权重。单峰 q 若夹在两个相隔很远的峰之间，会把大量概率放在 p 的低密度谷中；因此它常收窄并锁定一个峰：zero-forcing、mode-seeking，也叫 exclusive KL；称 I-projection / information projection

If q(x)>0 but p(x)=0, reverse KL is infinite. Averaging under q gives little weight to regions q rarely visits. A single q between two distant modes puts mass in p’s low-density valley, so it often narrows onto one mode: zero-forcing, mode-seeking, or exclusive KL. This is an I-projection, or information projection.

A tendency, not a universal rule: Gaussians are positive everywhere, so these curves have no exact zero-density region; very small densities create large penalties. With nearby modes, reverse KL can prefer a central approximation. If q is flexible enough to equal p, both objectives attain zero without sacrificing a mode.

最后记住这条线Keep this chain in mind

\[\text{Source }p\quad\longrightarrow\quad H(p)\quad\longrightarrow\quad H_{\mathrm{ce}}(p,q)=H(p)+D(p\parallel q)\]

熵看分布本身；交叉熵看预测代价；KL 看失配；联合熵与条件熵通过链式法则连接；困惑度是指数化的平均 log loss。MLE 在拟合经验数据。最后，forward 用 p 取平均，怕漏掉目标；reverse 用 q 取平均，怕把质量放错地方

Entropy measures the source; cross entropy measures prediction cost; KL measures mismatch. Joint and conditional entropy connect through the chain rule. Perplexity exponentiates average log loss. MLE fits empirical data. Forward averages under p and resists missing target regions; reverse averages under q and resists placing mass in implausible regions.
