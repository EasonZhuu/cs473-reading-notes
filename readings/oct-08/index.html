知道一个变量，能帮你多少？How much does one variable tell you about another?

上次我们问“一个分布有多不确定”和“模型偏离真实分布多少”。这次把问题换成：知道 Y 后，关于 X 的不确定性能减少多少？互信息把这件事量化，并进一步解释条件信息、加工信息的限度，以及怎样压缩数据却保留参数信息

Previously we measured a distribution’s uncertainty and a model’s mismatch. Now ask: how much does observing Y reduce uncertainty about X? Mutual information quantifies this, leading to conditional information, limits on data processing, and summaries that preserve parameter information.

6.3.1 · DEFINITION

把真实联合分布与“假装独立”比较Compare the joint distribution with an independence model

只看各自的边缘分布 p(x)、p(y)，你还不知道两个变量如何一起出现。如果它们独立，联合概率应是 p(x)p(y)。互信息用 KL 比较真实联合分布 p(x,y) 与这个独立模型：差异越明显，两个变量共享的信息越多

The marginals p(x) and p(y) do not specify how variables occur together. Independence would give p(x)p(y). Mutual information compares the actual joint p(x,y) to this independent model using KL: departures quantify shared information.

\[I(X;Y)=D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p(x,y)\parallel p(x)p(y)\right)=\sum_{x,y}p(x,y)\log\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}\]

一个比值大于 1 的组合，比独立模型预测的更常出现；小于 1 的组合更少出现。单项可以为负，完整平均却非负。约定 p(x,y)=0 的项贡献 0；只要联合概率为正，对应两个边缘概率也必然为正

A ratio above 1 means a pair appears more often than independence predicts; below 1 means less often. Individual contributions may be negative, but the complete average is nonnegative. Zero joint-probability terms contribute zero; positive joint probability implies positive corresponding marginals.

\[I(X;Y)\ge0,\qquad I(X;Y)=0\iff p(x,y)=p(x)p(y),\qquad I(X;Y)=I(Y;X)\]

KL 一般不对称，但这里交换 X、Y 只是交换同一个联合分布的坐标，所以互信息是对称的。连续变量把求和换成积分，非负性仍成立。log₂ 的单位是 bits，ln 的单位是 nats，bits=nats/ln 2

KL is generally asymmetric, but swapping X and Y only relabels the coordinates of the same joint, so mutual information is symmetric. For continuous variables, replace the sum with an integral; nonnegativity still holds. Base 2 gives bits and natural logs give nats, with bits=nats/ln 2.

互信息反映统计依赖，不等于因果关系。它也不告诉你关联的正负方向：完美正相关与完美负相关，都可以提供完整预测信息

Mutual information measures statistical dependence, not causation. It does not encode the sign of an association: perfect positive and perfect negative relationships can both provide complete predictive information.

6.3.2 · INTERPRETATION

互信息就是平均减少的不确定性Mutual information is average uncertainty reduction

\[I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)\]

H(X) 是看到 Y 之前的不确定性，H(X|Y) 是看过 Y 之后平均剩下的不确定性。两者之差就是学到了多少。由于互信息非负，也再次得到 H(X|Y)≤H(X)；这仍是平均结论，不保证每个特定观察都让你更确定

H(X) is uncertainty before observing Y; H(X|Y) is the average uncertainty remaining afterward. Their difference measures what you learned. Nonnegative MI implies H(X|Y)≤H(X), an average statement rather than a guarantee for every individual observation.

推导只需要熵的链式法则 H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X)。在 KL 定义中拆开 log[p(x,y)/(p(x)p(y))]，三个部分分别变成 −H(X,Y)、H(X)、H(Y)

Use the entropy chain rule H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X). Splitting the logarithm in the KL definition gives three terms: −H(X,Y), H(X), and H(Y).

\[I(X;Y)=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)\]
\[I(X;Y)=H(X,Y)-H(X\mid Y)-H(Y\mid X)\]

信息图把 H(X)、H(Y) 画成两个重叠区域：交集是 I(X;Y)，仅 X 的部分是 H(X|Y)，仅 Y 的部分是 H(Y|X)，并集是 H(X,Y)。这是离散变量的信息量示意，不是普通集合中“样本点”的交集；下面圆的面积不按数值比例缩放

An information diagram depicts H(X) and H(Y) as overlapping regions: the overlap is I(X;Y), the exclusive parts are H(X|Y) and H(Y|X), and the union is H(X,Y). These are information quantities for discrete variables, not intersecting sets of sample points. The circles below are schematic rather than scaled by area.

6.3.4 · CONDITIONAL MUTUAL INFORMATION

已经知道 Z，X 还能告诉你多少？After knowing Z, what extra information does X provide?

条件互信息不是把 Z “减掉”那么简单。它先在每个 Z=z 的条件分布内计算 X、Y 的互信息，然后按 p(z) 加权。它衡量的是在已有 Z 的情况下，X 对 Y 提供的额外信息

Conditional MI is not simply subtracting Z. Compute MI within each conditional distribution at Z=z, then average with p(z). It measures the extra information X provides about Y after Z is already known.

\[I(X;Y\mid Z)=\sum_zp(z)D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p(x,y\mid z)\parallel p(x\mid z)p(y\mid z)\right)\]
\[=\mathbb E_{p(x,y,z)}\!\left[\log\frac{p(x,y\mid z)}{p(x\mid z)p(y\mid z)}\right]\ge0\]
\[I(X;Y\mid Z)=H(X\mid Z)+H(Y\mid Z)-H(X,Y\mid Z)\]
\[=H(X\mid Z)-H(X\mid Y,Z)=H(Y\mid Z)-H(Y\mid X,Z)\]
联合熵形式 / Joint-entropy form
\[I(X;Y\mid Z)=H(X,Z)+H(Y,Z)-H(Z)-H(X,Y,Z)\]

用链式法则累计额外信息Accumulate additional information with the chain rule

\[I(Y;X,Z)=I(Y;Z)+I(Y;X\mid Z)\]
\[I(Z_1,\ldots,Z_N;X)=\sum_{n=1}^NI(Z_n;X\mid Z_1,\ldots,Z_{n-1})\]

先算 Z₁ 告诉你多少，再算已经知道 Z₁ 后 Z₂ 还能补充多少，依次累积。顺序可以改变每一项，但完整总量不变。I(X;Y|Z)=0 当且仅当 X、Y 在给定 Z 后条件独立（对 p(z)>0 的条件）

First count information from Z₁, then the extra contribution from Z₂ after Z₁, and continue. Reordering can change individual terms without changing the full total. I(X;Y|Z)=0 exactly when X and Y are conditionally independent given Z, for conditions with positive probability.

条件化可以降低，也可以提高互信息Conditioning can decrease or increase MI

Teaching examples: in a common-source model, X and Y are independently corrupted copies of Z. In XOR, balanced independent X and Z produce Y=X⊕Z, followed by a flip with probability ε. Compare ordinary and conditional MI.

没有一般规律 I(X;Y|Z)≤I(X;Y)。共同来源可以解释掉依赖；XOR 例子却有 I(X;Y)=0、I(X;Y|Z)=1。条件互信息不是简单“三个圆的公共交集”

There is no general rule I(X;Y|Z)≤I(X;Y). A common source can explain dependence away; noiseless XOR has I(X;Y)=0 but I(X;Y|Z)=1. Conditional MI is not simply the common intersection of three circles.

6.3.5 · GENERALIZED CORRELATION

高斯情形：相关越强，互信息越大For joint Gaussians, stronger correlation means more information

如果 X、Y 联合高斯，边缘方差都为 σ²，相关系数为 ρ，协方差矩阵的行列式是 σ⁴(1−ρ²)。把高斯熵代入 h(X)+h(Y)−h(X,Y)，共同的尺度项抵消，最终只剩 ρ

For jointly Gaussian X,Y with marginal variances σ² and correlation ρ, the covariance determinant is σ⁴(1−ρ²). Substituting Gaussian entropies into h(X)+h(Y)−h(X,Y) cancels the shared scale terms, leaving only ρ.

\[\Sigma=\sigma^2\begin{pmatrix}1&\rho\\\rho&1\end{pmatrix},\quad\det\Sigma=\sigma^4(1-\rho^2)\]
\[I(X;Y)=\frac12\ln\frac{\sigma^4}{\det\Sigma}=-\frac12\ln(1-\rho^2)\quad\mathrm{nats},\quad |\rho|<1\]

ρ=0 时互信息为 0，而且联合高斯下确实独立。ρ 的正负号不影响互信息；ρ→±1 时，关系趋近确定的连续映射，互信息趋向 ∞。这是无限精度连续变量的极限，不是“一个二元变量能告诉你无限 bits”

At ρ=0, MI is zero and joint Gaussianity implies independence. The sign of ρ does not change MI. As ρ approaches ±1, the relationship becomes deterministic and continuous MI diverges. This is an infinite-precision continuous limit, not infinite information from a binary variable.

6.3.8 · DATA PROCESSING INEQUALITY

加工信息，不能凭空增加源的信息Processing cannot create new information about the source

假设 X→Y→Z 是 Markov chain，也就是 p(x,y,z)=p(x)p(y|x)p(z|y)，给定 Y 后 X 与 Z 条件独立。Z 只能从 Y 生成，不能偷看 X 或得到额外侧信息。此时，无论加工方式是什么，Z 对 X 的互信息都不超过 Y

Assume X→Y→Z is a Markov chain: p(x,y,z)=p(x)p(y|x)p(z|y), so X and Z are conditionally independent given Y. Z is generated from Y without access to X or extra side information. Processing cannot increase MI with X.

\[I(X;Z)\le I(X;Y),\qquad I(X;Z)\le I(Y;Z)\]

证明：把同一信息量展开两次Proof: expand the same information in two orders

\[I(X;Y,Z)=I(X;Z)+I(X;Y\mid Z)=I(X;Y)+I(X;Z\mid Y)\]
\[I(X;Z\mid Y)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad I(X;Y)-I(X;Z)=I(X;Y\mid Z)\ge0\]

后处理可以把信息整理成更容易使用的表示，让某个有限能力的算法表现更好；但这不等于创造更多关于原始 X 的信息。若另外加入相关的侧信息，就需要重新判断 Markov 条件

Post-processing can reorganize information into a more useful representation and improve a limited algorithm’s performance. That does not create additional information about X. Adding relevant side information requires rechecking the Markov condition.

Teaching example: X is a fair bit. Y flips it with probability ε₁, then Z independently flips Y with probability ε₂. No second-stage noise preserves information; a 0.5 flip rate removes information about X.

DPI 不要求严格降低：可逆变换可以保留互信息，丢弃与 X 无关的冗余信息也可能保留。等号的条件是 I(X;Y|Z)=0；下一节的充分统计量正是这种“压缩但保留相关信息”的例子

DPI need not be strict: invertible transformations preserve MI, and removing information irrelevant to X may also preserve it. Equality holds when I(X;Y|Z)=0. Sufficient statistics provide exactly this kind of compression with relevant information preserved.

6.3.9 · SUFFICIENT STATISTICS

保留对参数有用的信息，丢掉其他细节Keep parameter information while discarding irrelevant detail

现在把源换成未知参数 θ，数据是 D，摘要是 s(D)。总有 θ→D→s(D)，所以摘要不能凭空增加关于 θ 的信息。如果它保留了全部相关信息，就称为 sufficient statistic

Let θ be an unknown parameter, D the data, and s(D) a summary. The chain θ→D→s(D) means summarizing cannot create information about θ. A summary that preserves all relevant information is a sufficient statistic.

\[I(\theta;s(D))\le I(\theta;D),\qquad\text{sufficiency: } I(\theta;s(D))=I(\theta;D)\]

在相应概率模型下，可以写成 θ ⟂ D | s(D)：给了摘要以后，原始数据不再补充参数信息。等价地，对于固定的先验和模型，p(θ|D)=p(θ|s(D))；频率学派的定义是数据在给定统计量后的条件分布不依赖 θ。这里 θ→s(D)→D 是概率生成关系，不是说能从摘要唯一恢复原始数据

Under the relevant probability model, sufficiency means θ ⟂ D | s(D): the original data add no parameter information once the summary is known. For a given prior and model, p(θ|D)=p(θ|s(D)); the frequentist definition says the conditional distribution of data given the statistic does not depend on θ. The chain θ→s(D)→D is probabilistic, not unique recovery of the original dataset.

Bernoulli：顺序可丢，成功次数要留Bernoulli trials: discard order, keep the success count

\[k=\sum_{n=1}^Nx_n,\qquad p(D\mid\theta)=\theta^k(1-\theta)^{N-k},\qquad s(D)=(N,k)\]

对独立同分布的 Bernoulli(θ) 试验，N 次中成功 k 次足以推断 θ。N 固定已知时只需 k。HHTT 与 HTHT 有相同 k，给 θ 的似然完全一样；若仅保留“有没有成功过”，就无法区分 1 次和 9 次成功，会丢信息

For iid Bernoulli(θ) trials, k successes out of N are sufficient for θ. If N is fixed and known, k alone suffices. HHTT and HTHT have identical likelihoods for θ. Keeping only whether any success occurred loses the distinction between one and nine successes.

充分不代表最精简Sufficient does not necessarily mean minimal

s(D)=D 当然充分，但没有压缩。Minimal sufficient statistic 要求对任何充分统计量 s′(D)，都存在函数 f，使 s(D)=f(s′(D))：任何充分摘要都必须能提供这个最小摘要。可概括为 θ→s(D)→s′(D)→D，其中更丰富的摘要包含冗余细节

s(D)=D is sufficient but does not compress. A minimal sufficient statistic satisfies s(D)=f(s′(D)) for every sufficient statistic s′: any sufficient summary must determine the minimal one. The relation can be summarized by θ→s(D)→s′(D)→D, with richer summaries retaining redundant details.

教材另一个例子：独立高斯样本、方差已知、只推断均值时，样本均值与样本数足够；N 固定时只需均值。充分性总是相对于模型和目标参数而言，换成未知方差或有时间依赖的试验，就要重新判断

The book’s other example: for iid Gaussian samples with known variance and an unknown mean, the empirical mean and sample count suffice; fixed N leaves just the mean. Sufficiency is always relative to the model and parameter of interest. Unknown variance or temporal dependence requires a new assessment.

MI measures dependence beyond an independence model; conditional MI measures extra information after Z. Joint Gaussians satisfy I=−½ln(1−ρ²), but zero correlation does not generally imply independence. Under the Markov condition, processing cannot create source information. Sufficient summaries preserve parameter information; minimal sufficient summaries remove redundancy.
