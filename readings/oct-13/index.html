OCT 13 · READING MAP

缺了标签，参数还怎么学？How do we learn when labels are hidden?

假设一批数据来自几个高斯分布，但你只看得到数值，不知道每个点来自哪一组。分组需要均值和方差，估计均值和方差又需要分组。EM 用两步交替打破这个循环：先计算“属于各组的概率”，再按这些概率重新估计参数。

Suppose observations come from several Gaussians, but their component labels are hidden. Assigning points needs means and variances, while estimating those parameters needs assignments. EM alternates two operations: calculate probabilities of membership, then re-estimate parameters using those probabilities.

E-step · 期望步固定参数，推断隐变量
Fix parameters; infer hidden variables
M-step · 最大化步固定责任度，更新参数
Fix responsibilities; update parameters
Lower bound · 下界先贴住目标，再向上走
Touch the objective, then improve

8.7.2 · THE PROBLEM

观测数据与完整数据，要分清Distinguish observed data from complete data

记 yₙ 为看到的数据，zₙ 为没有看到的标签或缺失值，θ 为要估计的参数。完整数据是 (yₙ,zₙ)。如果 zₙ 已知，每组的均值、方差和比例很容易算；zₙ 不知道时，我们必须把所有可能情况加起来。

Let yₙ be an observation, zₙ its hidden label or missing value, and θ the parameters to estimate. Complete data are (yₙ,zₙ). Known labels make component means, variances, and proportions easy to estimate. Hidden labels require summing over their possibilities.

\[p(y_n\mid\theta)=\sum_{z_n}p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta),\qquad \ell(\theta)=\sum_{n=1}^N\log\!\left[\sum_{z_n}p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta)\right]\]

困难在于“先求和，再取 log”。一般 log(a+b)≠log a+log b，不能直接把问题拆成各组独立的拟合。EM 优化的是这个观测数据的 log likelihood，而不是把猜出的标签当成真实标签。

The difficulty is the logarithm outside the sum. In general, log(a+b)≠log a+log b, so the objective does not separate into independent component fits. EM targets this observed-data log likelihood; it does not treat guessed labels as ground truth.

8.7.2.2 + 8.7.3.1 · E STEP

E-step：算概率，不做硬猜测E-step: infer probabilities instead of hard guesses

给定当前参数 θ⁽ᵗ⁾，计算每个隐变量的后验分布 qₙ⁽ᵗ⁾(zₙ)=p(zₙ|yₙ,θ⁽ᵗ⁾)。GMM 中，这个概率叫 responsibility（责任度）：这个成分对解释这个样本负责多少。每个样本的一行责任度之和为 1。

With the current parameters θ⁽ᵗ⁾ fixed, compute qₙ⁽ᵗ⁾(zₙ)=p(zₙ|yₙ,θ⁽ᵗ⁾). For a GMM, these posterior probabilities are responsibilities: how much each component accounts for a point. Each row of responsibilities sums to 1.

\[r_{nk}^{(t)}=p(z_n=k\mid y_n,\theta^{(t)})=\frac{\pi_k^{(t)}\,\mathcal N(y_n\mid\mu_k^{(t)},\Sigma_k^{(t)})}{\sum_{j=1}^K\pi_j^{(t)}\,\mathcal N(y_n\mid\mu_j^{(t)},\Sigma_j^{(t)})}\]

分子是“先属于 k 的比例 × k 产生这个数值的密度”，分母让各组加起来变成 1。πₖ 是观察样本之前的成分概率，rₙₖ 是看到 yₙ 之后的成分概率，二者不能混淆。高斯密度的值本身不是“属于该组的概率”。

The numerator combines the prior component weight with the density of producing this value. The denominator normalizes across components. πₖ is a component probability before observing yₙ; rₙₖ is its probability afterward. A Gaussian density value alone is not a membership probability.

E-step 不只计算 z 的均值再代入。需要的是完整数据 log likelihood 的期望，或足以计算它的期望充分统计量。一般 E[f(z)]≠f(E[z])。GMM 的 one-hot 标签 zₙₖ=I(zₙ=k) 满足 E[zₙₖ]=rₙₖ，所以责任度恰好够用。

Do not merely insert a mean value of z. EM needs the expectation of the complete-data log likelihood, or the expected sufficient statistics needed to compute it. In general, E[f(z)]≠f(E[z]). For a GMM, the one-hot indicator zₙₖ=I(zₙ=k) has expectation rₙₖ, so responsibilities suffice.

8.7.2.3 + 8.7.3.2 · M STEP

M-step：把软分组当作权重M-step: use soft assignments as weights

现在冻结本轮 E-step 算出的 q⁽ᵗ⁾，对参数 θ 最大化期望完整数据 log likelihood。冻结很关键：试不同 θ 时不要重新计算责任度，否则优化的就不是本轮的 M-step 目标。

Freeze q⁽ᵗ⁾ from the E-step and maximize the expected complete-data log likelihood over θ. Keeping q fixed is essential: recomputing responsibilities for each candidate θ changes the M-step objective.

\[\theta^{(t+1)}=\arg\max_\theta\sum_n\mathbb E_{q_n^{(t)}}\!\left[\log p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta)\right]\]

GMM 的期望完整目标就是加权 log likelihood。用 Nₖ 表示一个成分收到的“有效样本数”：一份样本可以贡献 0.8 份给第一组，0.2 份给第二组。Nₖ 可以不是整数。

For a GMM, the expected complete-data objective is a weighted log likelihood. Nₖ is a component’s effective sample count: a point can contribute 0.8 of a sample to one component and 0.2 to the other. Nₖ need not be an integer.

\[Q_{\mathrm{complete}}(\theta\mid\theta^{(t)})=\sum_n\sum_k r_{nk}^{(t)}\left[\log\pi_k+\log\mathcal N(y_n\mid\mu_k,\Sigma_k)\right]\]
\[N_k^{(t)}=\sum_n r_{nk}^{(t)},\qquad \pi_k^{(t+1)}=\frac{N_k^{(t)}}{N},\qquad \mu_k^{(t+1)}=\frac{\sum_n r_{nk}^{(t)}y_n}{N_k^{(t)}}\]
\[\Sigma_k^{(t+1)}=\frac{\sum_n r_{nk}^{(t)}(y_n-\mu_k^{(t+1)})(y_n-\mu_k^{(t+1)})^\top}{N_k^{(t)}}\]

均值是加权平均；协方差使用新均值来计算加权散布，除以 Nₖ，不是 Nₖ−1。这是最大似然更新。在一维中，协方差变成方差 σ²。两个成分的责任度逐点相加为 1，所以所有 Nₖ 相加为 N，所有新 πₖ 相加为 1。

The mean is a weighted average. The covariance measures weighted scatter around the new mean and divides by Nₖ, not Nₖ−1: this is a maximum-likelihood update. In 1D, covariance becomes variance σ². Since each point’s responsibilities sum to 1, effective counts sum to N and updated weights sum to 1.

GMM 只需累积 Σₙrₙₖ、Σₙrₙₖyₙ、Σₙrₙₖyₙyₙᵀ。教材一般指数族形式为 T(y,z)ᵀθ−A(θ)，取期望后，T 换成 E[T]；M-step 相当于用这些期望统计量做参数拟合。

For a GMM, accumulate Σₙrₙₖ, Σₙrₙₖyₙ, and Σₙrₙₖyₙyₙᵀ. In the book’s exponential-family form T(y,z)ᵀθ−A(θ), taking expectations replaces T with E[T]. The M-step fits parameters from those expected statistics.

\[\sum_n\mathbb E_q[\log p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta)]=\sum_n\left(\mathbb E_q[T(y_n,z_n)]^\top\theta-A(\theta)\right)\]

BASIC EM · THE WHOLE LOOP

把两步串起来，才是 EMEM is the repeated two-step loop

  1. 初始化合法的参数 θ⁽⁰⁾：混合比例为正且和为 1，协方差正定。

    Initialize valid parameters θ⁽⁰⁾: positive mixture weights summing to 1 and positive-definite covariances.

  2. E-step：固定 θ⁽ᵗ⁾，算隐变量后验或期望充分统计量。

    E-step: fix θ⁽ᵗ⁾ and infer posterior distributions or expected sufficient statistics.

  3. M-step：固定本轮后验，更新参数得到 θ⁽ᵗ⁺¹⁾。

    M-step: fix this iteration’s posteriors and update parameters to θ⁽ᵗ⁺¹⁾.

  4. 重新计算观测数据 log likelihood；检查改善量是否很小，并设置最大迭代次数。否则回到 E-step。

    Recompute the observed-data log likelihood. Check whether improvement is small and enforce an iteration limit; otherwise repeat the E-step.

8.7.1 · BOUND OPTIMIZATION

为什么不会越学越差？先看下界Why does the objective improve? Start with a lower bound

MM 在最大化问题中指 minorize-maximize：在当前位置 θ⁽ᵗ⁾ 构造一个处处不超过 ℓ(θ) 的替代函数 B(θ|θ⁽ᵗ⁾)，并要求它恰好碰到当前目标值。优化这个更容易的函数，然后在新位置重新构造下界。教材用 Q 表示这个包含常数项的完整下界，这里改称 B，以免与上面的期望完整目标混淆。

For maximization, MM means minorize-maximize. Construct a surrogate B(θ|θ⁽ᵗ⁾) below ℓ(θ) everywhere and touching it at the current point. Optimize that easier function, then rebuild the bound at the new point. The book calls this full bound Q; here B distinguishes it from the expected complete-data objective above.

\[B(\theta\mid\theta^{(t)})\le\ell(\theta),\qquad B(\theta^{(t)}\mid\theta^{(t)})=\ell(\theta^{(t)})\]
\[\ell(\theta^{(t+1)})\ge B(\theta^{(t+1)}\mid\theta^{(t)})\ge B(\theta^{(t)}\mid\theta^{(t)})=\ell(\theta^{(t)})\]

第一步来自“下界不能高于目标”，第二步来自“让下界升高”，最后一步来自“旧位置处贴紧”。因此目标非递减。最大化时是下界；最小化时 majorize-minimize 使用上界。二次下界看起来像 Newton 的二次近似，但普通近似不保证处处在正确一侧，可能过冲或降低目标。

The first inequality uses the lower-bound property, the second raises the bound, and the final equality uses tightness at the old point. Hence the objective is nondecreasing. Minimization uses an upper bound in majorize-minimize. A quadratic bound resembles a Newton approximation, but an ordinary approximation need not stay on the correct side and can overshoot or reduce the objective.

这是精确 E-step、正确 M-step 的检查工具：若观测似然明显下降，应检查数学、实现和数值计算。无需每次严格增加；收敛时可以相等。教材指出缺失信息量会影响收敛速度，所以很小的改善也可能意味着进展缓慢。

This is a diagnostic for exact E-steps and valid M-steps: a meaningful decrease in observed likelihood calls for checking the derivation, implementation, and numerics. Improvement need not be strict; equality is possible at convergence. The book notes that missing information affects convergence speed, so small improvements can also reflect slow progress.

8.7.2.1–8.7.2.3 · THE EM BOUND

EM 怎样把下界贴到似然上？How does EM make its bound touch the likelihood?

引入隐变量的分布 qₙ(zₙ)。利用 log 的凹性和 Jensen 不等式，得到 evidence lower bound（ELBO）。原来的“log 外面套求和”变成了“对 log joint 的加权平均”，再加 q 的熵。

Introduce distributions qₙ(zₙ). The concavity of log and Jensen’s inequality give an evidence lower bound, or ELBO. The logarithm of a sum becomes a weighted average of log joint probabilities, plus the entropy of q.

\[\ell(\theta)=\sum_n\log\mathbb E_{q_n}\!\left[\frac{p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta)}{q_n(z_n)}\right]\ge\sum_n\mathbb E_{q_n}[\log p(y_n,z_n\mid\theta)]+\sum_n H(q_n)\equiv\mathcal L(\theta,q)\]

最关键的关系不是背 Jensen，而是知道差距是什么：目标减去 ELBO，恰好是 q 与当前隐变量后验之间的 KL。若 q 与后验相同，KL=0，界就在当前 θ 贴紧。这里默认分布有适当支持，使期望与比值有定义。

The key is the gap: the likelihood minus the ELBO equals the KL divergence from q to the current posterior. When q equals that posterior, the KL is zero and the bound is tight at the current θ. The distributions are assumed to have appropriate support for the expectations and ratios.

\[\ell(\theta)-\mathcal L(\theta,q)=\sum_n D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q_n(z_n)\parallel p(z_n\mid y_n,\theta)\right)\ge0\]

E-step：θ 不动，把 q 换成当前后验，让界贴紧。M-step：q 不动，让 θ 提高这个界；由于 H(q) 对 θ 是常数，可在最大化时去掉。去掉熵不影响新 θ，但不能说只剩的期望完整目标与 ℓ 在旧点数值相等。

E-step: keep θ fixed and replace q with its posterior, making the bound tight. M-step: keep q fixed and improve the bound over θ. H(q) is constant in θ, so it can be omitted when finding the maximizer. Omitting entropy does not change the update, but the remaining expected complete-data objective alone need not numerically equal ℓ at the old point.
