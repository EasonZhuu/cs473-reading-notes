从最大间隔到核技巧From Margin to Kernel Trick

SVM 把分类写成“最大化最近样本到边界的距离”；对偶形式把权重改写为样本的加权和。核技巧再把特征空间内积替换为核函数调用。

An SVM maximizes the closest training-point distance. The dual rewrites its weights as a weighted sum of examples. The kernel trick replaces feature-space inner products with kernel evaluations.

Large marginDualSoft marginSupport vectorsMercer kernel

ONE IDEA / 一个核心想法

边界由少数样本支撑；核函数负责算相似度A few examples support the boundary; a kernel computes similarity

Margin / 间隔

离边界最近的点决定分类器能承受多大的扰动。

The nearest points set how much perturbation the classifier can tolerate.

Dual / 对偶

\(\mathbf w=\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n\mathbf x_n\)，预测只依赖非零系数的点。

Because \(\mathbf w=\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n\mathbf x_n\), prediction depends only on nonzero coefficients.

Kernel / 核

\(K(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=\phi(\mathbf x)^\top\phi(\mathbf x')\)：无需显式构造 \(\phi\) 也能算高维内积。

With \(K(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=\phi(\mathbf x)^\top\phi(\mathbf x')\), compute a high-dimensional dot product without constructing \(\phi\).

本次深度：正常理解 17.3.1；略讲 17.3.2、17.3.3；重点掌握 17.3.4 kernel trick。

Study depth: establish §17.3.1; briefly review §§17.3.2–17.3.3; focus on the §17.3.4 kernel trick.

17.3.1 · LARGE MARGIN CLASSIFIERS

先量最近样本离边界有多远Measure the nearest-point distance to the boundary

令标签 \(\tilde y_n\in\{-1,+1\}\)，分数 \(f(\mathbf x)=\mathbf w^\top\mathbf x+w_0\)，预测为 \(\mathrm{sign}(f)\)。点到边界的带符号距离为 \(f(\mathbf x)/\|\mathbf w\|\)，正确一侧的几何 margin 是 \(\tilde y_n f(\mathbf x_n)/\|\mathbf w\|\)。

Let \(\tilde y_n\in\{-1,+1\}\), score \(f(\mathbf x)=\mathbf w^\top\mathbf x+w_0\), and prediction \(\mathrm{sign}(f)\). Signed distance is \(f(\mathbf x)/\|\mathbf w\|\); the correctly oriented geometric margin is \(\tilde y_n f(\mathbf x_n)/\|\mathbf w\|\).

\[\min_{\mathbf w,w_0}\frac12\|\mathbf w\|^2\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \tilde y_n(\mathbf w^\top\mathbf x_n+w_0)\ge1\quad\forall n\]

缩放 \((\mathbf w,w_0)\) 不改变分界线，因此可把最近点的函数 margin 规范为 1。两条 margin 平面相距 \(2/\|\mathbf w\|\)。特征尺度很重要：单位变化会改变几何距离；使用 SVM 前应按训练方案缩放特征。

Rescaling \((\mathbf w,w_0)\) leaves the boundary unchanged, so normalize the closest function margin to 1. The two margin planes are \(2/\|\mathbf w\|\) apart. Feature scale matters: unit changes alter geometric distances, so scale features before fitting.

17.3.2 · THE DUAL PROBLEM · SKIM

对偶把权重写成样本的加权和The dual writes weights as a sum of examples

给每条 margin 约束引入 \(\alpha_n\ge0\)。驻点条件给出 \(\mathbf w=\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n\mathbf x_n\)、\(\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n=0\)。因此对偶目标只包含样本两两内积：

Associate \(\alpha_n\ge0\) with each margin constraint. Stationarity gives \(\mathbf w=\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n\mathbf x_n\) and \(\sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n=0\). The dual objective then contains only pairwise inner products:

\[\max_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\sum_n\alpha_n-\frac12\sum_i\sum_j\alpha_i\alpha_j\tilde y_i\tilde y_j\,\mathbf x_i^\top\mathbf x_j,\qquad \alpha_n\ge0,\quad \sum_n\alpha_n\tilde y_n=0\]
TermShort reading / 简明理解
Primal优化 \(\mathbf w,w_0\)，逐个约束样本离边界至少一个规范化单位。Optimizes \(\mathbf w,w_0\), with one normalized-margin constraint per example.
Dual优化 \(N\) 个系数，目标只使用标签和成对内积。Optimizes \(N\) coefficients using labels and pairwise inner products.
KKT sparsity\(\alpha_n(\tilde y_nf(x_n)-1)=0\)：\(\alpha=0\) 的点不参与；\(\alpha>0\) 的点在 margin 上，叫 support vector。\(\alpha_n(\tilde y_nf(x_n)-1)=0\): zero-alpha points drop out; positive-alpha points lie on the margin and are support vectors.

教材给通用二次规划 \(O(N^3)\)，并提到专用 SMO 约 \(O(N)\) 到 \(O(N^2)\)。本节的关键是：训练和预测只需内积。

The text gives \(O(N^3)\) for generic QP and about \(O(N)\)–\(O(N^2)\) for specialized SMO. The key setup is that training and prediction need only inner products.

17.3.3 · SOFT MARGIN · SKIM

允许违约，但把违约计入代价Allow violations, but charge for them

当数据不可线性分开时，引入 slack \(\xi_n\ge0\)，允许样本进入 margin 或被错分。参数 \(C\) 决定总 slack 的惩罚强度。

When data are not linearly separable, slack \(\xi_n\ge0\) lets points enter the margin or be misclassified. \(C\) controls the penalty on total slack.

\[\min_{\mathbf w,w_0,\boldsymbol\xi}\frac12\|\mathbf w\|^2+C\sum_n\xi_n\quad\text{s.t.}\quad\tilde y_n(\mathbf w^\top\mathbf x_n+w_0)\ge1-\xi_n\]

对偶变量受限于 \(0\le\alpha_n\le C\)。\(\alpha=0\) 表示不参与；\(0<\alpha<C\) 表示恰在 margin；\(\alpha=C\) 的点可在 margin 内或错分。ν-SVM 用 \(\nu\) 控制 margin error 比例，并给 support vector 数量下界。

The dual coefficients satisfy \(0\le\alpha_n\le C\). \(\alpha=0\) is inactive; \(0<\alpha<C\) lies on the margin; \(\alpha=C\) may be inside the margin or misclassified. A \(\nu\)-SVM controls the fraction of margin errors and lower-bounds the support-vector count.

3

调节 C，看对偶系数是否触顶Adjust C and watch the dual coefficients hit their bound

精确可解的教学例子：\(x=\{-2,-1,1,2\}\)，外侧正类、内侧负类，\(\phi(x)=(x,x^2)\)。对称解为每个 \(\alpha^*=\min(C,1/9)\)。

An exactly solvable teaching example: \(x=\{-2,-1,1,2\}\), outer points positive and inner points negative, with \(\phi(x)=(x,x^2)\). Symmetry gives \(\alpha^*=\min(C,1/9)\) for each point.

Here \(\|\mathbf w\|=6\alpha\), each signed margin score is \(9\alpha\), and slack is \(\max(0,1-9\alpha)\). At small \(C\), \(\alpha=C\); above the threshold, slack vanishes.

17.3.4 · THE KERNEL TRICK · FOCUS

把高维内积换成一次 kernel 调用Replace a high-dimensional dot product with a kernel call

若 \(K(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=\phi(\mathbf x)^\top\phi(\mathbf x')\)，就能在不显式构造 \(\phi\) 的情况下，算出映射空间内积。这就是 kernel trick。SVM 的对偶训练和预测只用内积，所以可以逐项替换。

If \(K(\mathbf x,\mathbf x')=\phi(\mathbf x)^\top\phi(\mathbf x')\), compute the mapped-space dot product without constructing \(\phi\). This is the kernel trick. Since dual SVM training and prediction use only inner products, substitute the kernel throughout.

\[f(\mathbf x)=\sum_{n\in S}\alpha_n\tilde y_nK(\mathbf x_n,\mathbf x)+w_0,\qquad \hat w_0=\frac1{|S|}\sum_{i\in S}\left[\tilde y_i-\sum_{j\in S}\alpha_j\tilde y_jK(\mathbf x_j,\mathbf x_i)\right]\]
1 · Map

概念上进入特征空间 \(\phi(\mathbf x)\)。

Conceptually map the input into feature space.

2 · Inner product

对偶只需要特征向量两两内积。

The dual needs pairwise feature inner products.

3 · Kernel call

直接算 \(K\)，即可用高维线性边界形成输入空间的非线性边界。

Evaluate \(K\) directly to create a nonlinear input-space boundary from a linear feature-space boundary.

不是任意相似度都合法。Mercer kernel 的 Gram matrix 对称且半正定：任意 \(\mathbf a\) 都有 \(\mathbf a^\top K\mathbf a\ge0\)。这确保 \(K\) 对应某个特征空间内积。

Not every similarity is valid. A Mercer Gram matrix is symmetric positive semidefinite: \(\mathbf a^\top K\mathbf a\ge0\) for every \(\mathbf a\). This ensures an underlying feature-space inner product.

对偶仍有 \(N\) 个系数；kernel trick 省去显式 \(D\) 维坐标，还能处理字符串、图等结构化对象。若 \(N\) 很大，优化仍可能昂贵；它提供隐式特征计算，不保证每种数据都更快。

The dual still has \(N\) coefficients. The trick avoids explicit \(D\)-dimensional coordinates and supports structured objects such as strings and graphs. Large \(N\) can remain expensive; the trick enables implicit features, not a speedup guarantee for every dataset.

Positives are at ±2 and negatives at ±1. The map \(\phi(x)=(x,x^2)\) makes them linearly separable; \(K(x,x')=xx'+x^2x'^2\) evaluates that feature-space dot product directly.

TAKEAWAYS / 带走这几句

找到最近的点，再省去高维坐标Find the nearest points, then avoid high-dimensional coordinates

最大间隔是几何优化。对偶让权重成为样本加权和，KKT 选出 support vectors。软间隔以 \(\xi,C\) 处理不可分数据。最后，用半正定 kernel 替代全部特征空间内积，无需显式构造 \(\phi(x)\)。

Maximum margin is geometric optimization. The dual expresses weights as a sum over examples, and KKT conditions select support vectors. Soft margins use \(\xi,C\) for nonseparable data. A positive-semidefinite kernel replaces every feature-space inner product without explicitly constructing \(\phi(x)\).
